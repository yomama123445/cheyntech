About CheynTech

Connecting Filipinos to the Gadgets They Love

OUR STORY

Born Local, Built on Trust

CheynTech — trading as Cheyn's Gadgets — started as a small passion project right here in Molo, Iloilo City. What began as reselling a few pre-owned iPhones among friends quickly grew into a community-trusted shop for Ilonggos looking for quality gadgets at fair prices.

Today, we carry a curated selection of pre-owned, refurbished, and brand-new smartphones and tablets — every unit personally checked before listing. We believe every Filipino deserves access to a great device without overpaying.

Our Mission

To make reliable, quality gadgets accessible and affordable for every Filipino — with transparency, honesty, and genuine after-sales care.

WHY CHOOSE US

Shopping at CheynTech is Different

We hold ourselves to a higher standard — because you deserve more than just a transaction.

Quality Checked

Every single unit — pre-owned or refurbished — is tested and inspected before it ever gets listed. Battery health, screen, ports, and camera are all verified.

Honest Pricing

What you see is what you pay — no hidden fees, no surprise charges at checkout. We price fairly based on actual market value and unit condition.

7-Day Guarantee

If your unit shows any defect or issue within 7 days of delivery, we'll replace it — no questions asked. Your peace of mind comes first.

THE TEAM

Small Team, Big Heart

Behind CheynTech is a small, dedicated team of gadget enthusiasts right here in Iloilo City. We're not a big corporation — we're your neighbors, and we genuinely care about every order we pack and every inquiry we receive.

Our team handles everything from sourcing and quality-checking units, to packing orders and coordinating delivery through trusted local couriers. We personally respond to all messages and inquiries, because good customer service isn't just a policy — it's who we are.

Whether you're buying your first smartphone or upgrading to a newer model, we're here to help you make the right choice — honestly and without pressure.
