CheynTech

Online Ordering and Delivery Management System for Cheyn’s Gadgets

Group 3 | AY 2026–2027

Problem Statement

  • No online sales channel and lack of systemized inventory tracking.
  • No digital product catalog, making it difficult for customers to browse remotely.
  • No remote purchasing or official delivery options.
  • Untracked stockouts due to informal inventory management.
  • Missing transaction records causing difficult financial tracking.

Objective

  • Develop a fully functional, live-deployed web platform for ordering and delivery.
  • Provide a customer-facing storefront for browsing and checkout.
  • Offer order fulfillment via in-store pickup or local delivery.
  • Implement real-time inventory tracking and status updates.
  • Equip the owner with an admin dashboard for product and order management.
